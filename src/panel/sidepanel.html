Scoring Methodology
01
Claim Decomposition

Input text split into atomic verifiable statements without using AI knowledge as a truth source.

02
Evidence Retrieval

Wikipedia (encyclopedic) + SerpAPI (live web) queried per claim before any verdict is formed.

03
NLI Classification

LLM classifies each claim↔source passage relationship: SUPPORT, CONTRADICT, or NO_DECISIVE_EVIDENCE.

04
Deterministic Aggregation

Confidence = Σ(weight × SUPPORT) ÷ Σ(weight × decisive) × 100. Zero decisive evidence → N/A, never fabricated.
